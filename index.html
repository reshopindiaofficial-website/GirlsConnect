How it works

Share GirlsConnect with your friends on WhatsApp. Every friend who joins with your link or referral code earns you ₹1 and keeps your profile at the top for 1 hour. For example, 3 friends = 3 hours, 10 friends = 10 hours. Invite 99 friends to get the Winner ✔ badge for good.

  • Withdraw once your balance reaches ₹100. There is no maximum limit, and you can withdraw once every 28 days.
  • When your top time ends, your profile goes back to its normal place.
  • The profile whose friend joined most recently stays at the very top, then the other boosted profiles, then the rest of the members.
  • Only new, genuine members are counted. The same phone or mobile number is never counted twice.
